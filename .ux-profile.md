# Koba UX profile

## Aesthetic reference
Quiet luxury hospitality. Cinematic, candlelit. The brand film is the reference for every surface.
Motion is Apple-leaning: up to 300ms, always purposeful. One orchestrated moment (the hero entrance).

## Accessibility target
WCAG 2.1 AA. Tap targets at least 44x44px. Visible 2px gold focus ring with 2px offset in both themes.

## Voice
Warm, calm, confident, short. Sentence case.
No em dashes. No filler: thrilled, excited, passionate, elevate, curated experience.
Amharic labels stay exactly as written.

## Banned
- Glassmorphism and backdrop blur
- Rainbow or multi-hue gradients
- Cartoon illustrations
- Decorative pills
- ALL-CAPS labels above headings (the KOBA wordmark is the only all-caps text)
- Middle-dot meta strings
- Arrows appended to buttons
- Numbered section markers

## Icons
Simple line icons, gold or ivory, functional only.
